What to automate first in your real estate business

The answer is your first response, because it's the only automation that pays off even if you change every other tool later. Everything else — sequences, pipelines, AI — builds on top of it.

This page is a pure checklist. There are no affiliate links here; use it with whatever tools you already own, and come back to automating a real estate business when you're ready to pick a platform.

Week 1: stop the bleeding

  1. Missed-call text-back. Every missed call triggers an automatic text: "Sorry I missed you — are you looking to buy or sell?" Setup guide here.
  2. Instant form reply. Every website and portal lead gets an immediate text or email acknowledging them and offering a time to talk.
  3. After-hours auto-reply. A clear hour window when leads are told exactly when they'll hear from you.

Week 2: one home for every lead

  1. Pick one CRM and route every source into it — Zillow, Realtor.com, website, referrals. See our real estate CRM guide.
  2. Tag every lead with its source the moment it enters. No tag, no lead.
  3. Build three pipeline stages minimum: New → Contacted → Appointment. Don't over-build.

Week 3: the follow-up engine

  1. Install a 14-day follow-up sequence for new leads that don't respond.
  2. Add a long-term nurture (monthly) for leads who say "maybe in 6–12 months."
  3. Create a task automation: no response after the sequence → a human call task, not more automation.

Week 4 and beyond: the AI layer

  1. Add a website chatbot only if your site gets real traffic.
  2. Consider an AI receptionist only if missed-call text-back proves you're losing real volume.
  3. Track speed to lead weekly; it's the metric that tells you if any of this is working.

FAQ

What is the first thing a realtor should automate?

The automatic first reply. It's the only automation that pays off with any tool stack.

Should I automate before or after choosing a CRM?

Instant responses first (they work almost anywhere), then make the CRM the home for everything those responses capture.

How long does this whole checklist take?

Realistically two to four weeks if you do a piece each week — not a weekend, and that's fine.
