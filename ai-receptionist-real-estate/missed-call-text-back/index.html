Missed call text back for real estate: the cheapest lead you already paid for

The short answer: missed-call text-back is the highest-ROI automation in real estate — because a caller who gets a text within seconds of your missed call often continues the conversation instead of dialing the next agent on Google. Setup takes an afternoon, not a project.

How it works

  1. A call comes in. You're in a showing, and it goes to your business number.
  2. Within seconds, an automated text fires: acknowledgment + one easy question.
  3. Their reply lands in your CRM as a two-way conversation — with a source tag and a follow-up task.

Setup checklist

  1. Get a texting-capable business number (CRM platform or a dedicated texting tool).
  2. Forward or route missed calls from your cell to that number.
  3. Enable the missed-call text-back automation with the script below.
  4. Make sure replies create a task for a human callback — the text opens the door, you walk through it.
  5. Test it: call yourself from a friend's phone and watch the full path.

Scripts that get replies

Version A (general): "Hi, this is [Name] — sorry I missed your call, I'm with a client. Are you looking to buy or sell? I can call you back this afternoon. Reply STOP to opt out."

Version B (sign/property calls): "Thanks for calling about the property! Sorry I couldn't pick up. Want photos, price, or a showing time? Text back and I'll get it to you. Reply STOP to opt out."

Rules: one question, one promised callback window, always an opt-out. Don't stack three questions.

FAQ

Does missed call text back annoy callers?

Rarely — it's immediate, relevant, and easy to ignore. One message, one opt-out.

What should the text say?

Acknowledge the miss and ask one easy question — see the scripts above.

Is automated texting legal for real estate?

With proper disclosures and opt-out handling, generally yes — but rules vary by state. Confirm with your broker/attorney.
