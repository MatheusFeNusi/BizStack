Website chatbot for realtors: scope, scripts, and setup

The short answer: a good real estate chatbot captures contact info, answers ten narrow questions, and offers one next step — because every capability beyond that adds failure modes without adding leads. This page is a no-affiliate setup guide.

Define the scope before the tool

  1. Capture: name + phone or email, buy/sell, timeline, area.
  2. Answer: areas served, your process, how to book a call — all from a knowledge base you write.
  3. Advance: offer exactly one CTA per conversation (book a call OR get listings OR request a valuation).

Opening scripts that work

General: "Hi! I'm the automated assistant for [Name]'s team. I can help with area info or get you in touch — are you looking to buy, sell, or just browsing?"

Listing pages: "Questions about this property? I can share details or set up a time to see it. What's the best number to reach you?"

Rules: disclose it's automated, one question at a time, and always end with a human promise ("[Name] will follow up personally").

Routing rules that make it valuable

  1. Every chat → your CRM, tagged website-chat. Compare against your other sources monthly.
  2. Hot intent (wants a showing / valuation) → instant notification to you, not just the CRM.
  3. Everything else → enters your standard follow-up sequence.
  4. Unread chats after 15 minutes during business hours → escalate to a human reply.

Common mistakes

  • Letting it free-answer about pricing, schools, or neighborhoods — Fair Housing and accuracy risk in one box.
  • Asking for email before offering anything. Value first, contact info second.
  • Never reading transcripts. Monthly transcript review is where the bot actually improves.

FAQ

What should it say first?

Disclose it's automated, then ask one question — buy, sell, or browsing.

24/7 or after hours only?

24/7 is fine; the speed of human handoff during work hours is what matters.

Which pages need the widget?

Start with listing/property pages and your contact page — highest intent. Homepage optional.
