Real estate follow-up sequence: a 14-day template you can copy

The short answer: new leads need 14 days of mixed-channel touches with stop-on-reply — because most replies happen after the first few attempts, and persistence only works if it switches channels. This page is a no-affiliate template; adapt the wording to your voice.

The rules before the sequence

  • Stop-on-reply is mandatory. Any response removes the lead from automation and creates a human task.
  • Mix channels. Text, call task, email — vary it. Same-channel repetition reads as spam.
  • Every message earns its send by offering something: a listing, an answer, a time slot.
  • After day 14: non-responders move to a monthly nurture, not the trash.

The sequence

DayChannelMessage skeleton
0 (instant)Text + email"Thanks for reaching out about [source/property] — I'm [Name]. When's a good time for a quick call this week?"
1Call taskHuman call. No answer → voicemail referencing the property, then text: "Just tried you — [one specific detail]. Better time tomorrow?"
3EmailValue email: 3 listings matching their criteria, or "how buying/selling in [area] works right now." One CTA: book a call.
5TextOne-question text: "Still looking in [area], or did plans change?" Binary questions get replies.
7Call taskHuman call at a different time of day than day 1.
10EmailProof email: a recent client's process walkthrough (no fabricated testimonials — describe the process, not a fake quote) + booking CTA.
14TextThe honest close: "I'll stop bugging you — want me to send monthly market updates for [area] instead? Reply YES or STOP."

Long-term nurture (day 15+)

One email a month: market snapshot for their area or a useful guide. One text per quarter, max, referencing something real. The goal is to be the obvious name when their timeline changes — most real estate timelines do.

FAQ

What is stop-on-reply?

Any response removes the lead from automation and assigns a human task. The most important anti-robot safeguard.

What if they reply "not interested"?

Remove them immediately and log it. Opting people out fast protects your deliverability and your reputation.

Should buyer and seller leads use the same sequence?

Same skeleton, different specifics — swap the listing email for a valuation report for sellers, and tune the one-question texts accordingly.
