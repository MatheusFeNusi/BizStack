Lead follow-up for real estate: the system that actually pays

The short answer: follow-up is the highest-leverage thing you can automate — because most commissions are lost in the gap between "lead arrives" and "agent responds," not in lead quality. Build speed first, sequence second, and let humans handle replies.

The two truths of real estate follow-up

  1. Speed beats polish. A fast, decent reply outperforms a perfect one sent tomorrow. The mechanics live in our speed-to-lead guide.
  2. Persistence beats intensity. Most deals come from leads who didn't reply to the first three touches. That's what the follow-up sequence is for.

The order of operations

  1. Instant auto-reply on every channel: forms, portals, chat, and missed calls.
  2. Personal touch within the hour during waking hours — a call or a one-to-one text.
  3. Install the 14-day sequence from our template for non-responders. Stop-on-reply, always.
  4. Shift survivors to monthly nurture instead of dropping them into the "dead" folder.
  5. Measure monthly: response time, reply rate by touch, appointments per 100 leads.

The rules that keep it human

  • Automation opens doors; it never argues through them. Any reply → conversation leaves the sequence and a human takes over.
  • Every message references something real: the property, the source, the question they asked.
  • Opt-outs honored instantly, everywhere. Non-negotiable for deliverability and for compliance.

FAQ

How fast should you follow up?

Instant automated reply; personal touch within the hour; structured sequence after that.

How many follow-ups is too many?

In the first two weeks, a structured multi-touch sequence is appropriate — then move non-responders to monthly nurture rather than stopping or spamming.

Can it be automated without sounding robotic?

Yes: automation opens, humans close. Every reply is routed to a real person immediately.

Which channel works best: text, call, or email?

Text for the first reply, calls for serious intent, email for long-tail nurture. Sequences should mix all three.

What about old leads sitting in my database?

A reactivation campaign (short, honest, one clear CTA) is the cheapest lead source you own — after instant response is fixed.
