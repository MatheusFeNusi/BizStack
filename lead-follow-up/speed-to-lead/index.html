Speed to lead in real estate: how fast is fast enough

The short answer: your first response should be automatic and instant; your personal touch should land within the hour — because lead intent decays with every unanswered minute, and no amount of follow-up quality fully recovers a lead that already moved on. This page has no affiliate links; it's the measurement guide.

What to actually measure

  • Time to first response — per channel (form, portal, missed call, chat). Automation should make every channel effectively instant.
  • Time to personal touch — the first human call or one-to-one text. Target: within the hour during waking hours.
  • Reply rate by channel — tells you where the conversation actually happens (in most markets: text).
  • Appointments per 100 leads — the only number that pays the bills.

Why "I call back same day" isn't a system

Same-day is a promise you break every time you have three showings in a row, a closing, or a weekend. Speed to lead fails at exactly the moments you're busiest — which are also the moments the most leads arrive. That's the argument for automation: it covers the floor so your time defines the ceiling.

Getting first response under a minute

  1. Forms and portals: instant text + email auto-reply wired to lead creation.
  2. Phone: missed-call text-back on every unanswered call.
  3. Chat: instant bot greeting that captures contact info (setup guide).
  4. All replies → push notification to you. Instant reply is worthless if the reply sits unread for three hours.

Where humans must take over

Automation wins the first minute; humans win everything after. A lead who replied is no longer a speed-to-lead problem — they're a conversation. Route every reply out of the sequence (stop-on-reply) and into your follow-up workflow's human tasks.

A weekly 10-minute review

  1. Pull the week's leads. What was the slowest first response, and why?
  2. Which channel generated replies fastest? Shift attention there.
  3. Any lead with zero automated response = a broken integration. Fix it this week, not someday.

FAQ

What is speed to lead?

The time between a lead arriving and the first response they receive — the most controllable variable in conversion.

Is an auto-reply "good enough" as a first response?

It holds the lead; it doesn't close them. Pair it with a personal touch within the hour.

Does speed matter for referrals too?

Yes — referred buyers and sellers still judge responsiveness, even if they're more patient.
