GoHighLevel setup for real estate, step by step

The short answer: set up lead capture and pipelines before any automation — because workflows that trigger on missing or mis-tagged leads are the #1 reason new users think "it doesn't work." This guide is the weekend plan, in order.

Before you start

You'll need an active GoHighLevel account. Don't import your old spreadsheet of 2,000 contacts yet — the system earns that import once it's alive.

Day 1 morning: pipes and phone

  1. Provision your number. Settings → Phone Numbers → buy a local-area-code number. This powers texting and missed-call text-back.
  2. Create your pipeline. Stages: New Lead → Attempted Contact → In Conversation → Appointment Set → Under Contract. Resist adding more.
  3. Create source tags. zillow, realtor-com, website, referral, sign-call. Every lead will carry one.

Day 1 afternoon: capture every lead

  1. Website forms. Replace or route existing forms into GHL (native forms or webhook from your current site).
  2. Portal leads. Connect Zillow/Realtor.com via email parsing or Zapier into the New Lead stage with the right source tag.
  3. Test with yourself. Submit a real lead from each source before moving on. No test, no launch.

Day 2 morning: instant response

  1. Missed-call text-back. Enable it on your number with a real-estate-specific message. Full rationale in our missed-call text-back guide.
  2. New-lead instant reply. Workflow: trigger = lead created → send text + email within 1 minute, personalized with name and source.

Day 2 afternoon: the nurture workflow

  1. Build the 14-day sequence from our follow-up sequence template as a workflow with stop-on-reply.
  2. Add the escape hatch: any reply → stop automation → create a manual call task.
  3. Send yourself through the whole sequence before any real lead sees it.

What to skip in week one

  • Funnels and landing pages — your existing site is fine for now.
  • Calendars, reputation management, memberships — powerful later, noise now.
  • Importing your entire database. Import only active conversations first.

FAQ

How long does setup take?

One focused weekend for the core system; you'll refine workflows over the following weeks.

Do I have to port my phone number?

No — provision a new number in-app and route missed calls from your existing number to it.

Can I migrate from Follow Up Boss or LionDesk?

Yes, via CSV export/import — but import active leads first and archive-history second.
