Follow Up Boss: the turnkey pick (no affiliate link)

Who it's for: teams that want a CRM that understands real estate on day one — native Zillow/Realtor.com lead routing, agent accountability reports.

Who should skip it: anyone hoping it replaces email marketing or landing pages — it won't; you'll buy those separately, and total stack cost climbs past GoHighLevel quickly.

LionDesk: the simple pick (no affiliate link)

Who it's for: solo agents who want texting, a dialer, and drip campaigns at a low price with minimal setup.

Who should skip it: teams planning real automation — the workflow builder is basic, and you'll outgrow it the moment you want conditional logic or multi-channel sequences.

FAQ

What's the best CRM for a solo realtor?

GoHighLevel if you'll automate follow-up and want one login; a simpler CRM if you only want contacts and reminders.

Is Follow Up Boss better than GoHighLevel?

Easier and more real-estate-native out of the box; GoHighLevel bundles more tools and deeper automation at a similar price.

Should I switch CRMs if mine "works"?

Only if you're missing automated follow-up or two-way texting. Switching CRMs for features you won't configure is how agents end up with three half-used tools.
