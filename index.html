How to automate a real estate business — in the right order

Most agents don't have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem: calls missed while showing homes, website forms answered hours later, and a CRM nobody keeps updated. BizStack maps out what to automate first, second, and never — for solo agents and teams up to 10 people.

Everything here is organized around one principle: fix response speed before you buy more leads, and fix your CRM before you add AI on top of it. Start with the cluster below that matches your biggest leak.

Solo agents, brokers, and teams of up to 10 who already have leads coming in — and are losing them to slow follow-up. If you're drowning in disconnected tools, the goal here is to help you pick fewer, not more.
